# Runninggem product brief

## Principle

Less noise. More running. The athlete should see the next useful action, while deeper information stays one tap away.

## Navigation

Exactly three primary tabs:

| Tab | Athlete question | Initial behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Today | What should I do today? | Current session, weekly adherence, next session |
| Calendar | What is planned and what have I done? | Weekly agenda, previous/next week, rest days, workout details |
| Performance | How is my training adding up? | Week/month totals, volume chart, sample records, recent sessions |

## Reference interpretation

The supplied Runna views provide the foundation for the calendar agenda, activity summary cards and volume/record hierarchy. The supplied Garmin Today screen reinforces the need for a focused current-session view. Avoid porting the five-tab navigation or exposing all metrics at once.

Use original code, typography, simple stroke icons and a distinct cream/charcoal/lime visual system. No Runna or Garmin assets are bundled. Athlete photographs and personal screenshots are deliberately excluded from the repository.

## Interactions in v0.1

- View the details of any listed workout.
- Save completion, effort (1–10) and a note locally.
- See completion reflected consistently in all three views.
- Move through calendar weeks and return to the fixed demo week.
- Switch weekly/monthly performance summaries.

## Name

**Runninggem**, chosen by the owner during bootstrap. App identifier and proposed repository name: `runninggem`. Trademark and domain availability have not been checked. Visual wordmark can evolve later; this is not a final brand identity.

## Follow-up milestones

1. Owner reviews athlete screens and identifies the most important daily action.
2. Usability sessions with three runners, preferably including one less technical athlete.
3. Authentication, durable workout/feedback persistence and athlete isolation.
4. Assisted coach assignment and basic coach review, then one approved wearable integration.
5. Paid pilot, measured retention and coach time savings.
