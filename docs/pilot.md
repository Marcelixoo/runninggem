# Small paid pilot

## Intended shape

Three coaches, 30–60 total athletes, four to six weeks. Start with the athlete experience; coach authoring and onboarding can initially be assisted manually after a minimal protected backend exists. Do not charge for this static prototype as if it already delivers live coaching or sync.

## Hypothesis

Athletes can understand today's session and submit useful feedback without learning a complex dashboard. Coaches save enough time reviewing adherence and effort to pay for the workflow.

## Recruitment

Interview 8–12 coaches first. Ask to observe a real training week, including assignment, rescheduling, feedback and missed sessions. Record the baseline in minutes rather than asking whether a simpler app sounds appealing.

## Provisional success criteria

These are proposed experiment thresholds, not industry benchmarks:

- Three coaches commit to a paid pilot at an agreed price.
- At least 70% of invited athletes activate in the first week.
- At least 60% of scheduled sessions receive completion or feedback during the pilot.
- Coaches report and demonstrate at least 30 minutes saved per week.
- At least two of three coaches renew after the pilot.
- Capture missing sync, coach editing and migration features as explicit reasons for non-adoption.

## Illustrative economics

Pricing hypothesis: R$199 per coach per month, including 30 active athletes. Not validated; define extra-athlete economics before scaling.

| Paying coaches | MRR | Annualized revenue |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 25 | R$4,975 | R$59,700 |
| 100 | R$19,900 | R$238,800 |
| 300 | R$59,700 | R$716,400 |
| 500 | R$99,500 | R$1,194,000 |

Planning assumption: R$5,000–15,000/month early operating budget excluding founder compensation. This is a deliberately broad business allowance, not a quoted hosting bill. Split it into infrastructure, payment costs, accounting, support, API charges and acquisition as actual quotes become available.

At an assumed 85% contribution margin, R$10,000 fixed monthly costs require 60 coaches; R$40,000 requires 237. Formula: ceiling(fixed costs / (199 × .85)). These are operating break-even illustrations before any taxes or costs omitted from the assumptions. Avoid counting an expense twice across variable and fixed costs.

## Before taking payment

Implement user accounts, isolated athlete data, durable storage, export/deletion, reliable recovery and basic support. Confirm integration terms. Define exactly what the pilot includes, its price, duration, cancellation/refund terms and data handling. Determine an appropriate Brazilian billing and privacy setup with qualified help as necessary.

## Boundaries

No autonomous medical advice, injury diagnosis or automated training prescription in this pilot. No social feed, public marketplace, indoor virtual world or broad sport coverage. Keep founder hours bounded and track support time per coach.
